Welcome To Ottocraft!|===========================::1
You begin in an ordinary Overworld with this book, a torch, a coffee and a slice of toast. The book follows the original SkyFactory 3 ages - tools, farming, machines, magic, power and dimensions - rebuilt for a normal world, together with Create, the Twilight Forest and the Aether.::1
How To Use This Book|===================::1
Use the page arrows to browse and tick a challenge after completing it. Each age explains when to begin and what it opens next. Every challenge in an age can be finished with what earlier ages taught you. Keep an Achievement Stand at home for your book.::1
Your World|==========::1
Ores, trees, villages and structures generate normally. The SkyFactory sifting tools - Crooks, Sieves, Hammers and Barrels - are still here as renewable resource routes for when caves run dry. Use them alongside mining, not instead of it.::1
JEI shows every recipe in this pack. Use it to trace a missing ingredient back to a farm, machine, structure or boss.::1
|Also, if you like training dragons, remember that the biome you hatch your dragon in decides what kind of dragon it becomes!::1
